# TEASER 1: “SOMETHING IS COMING…”

**Concept:** Mystery, suspense, and anticipation  
**Duration:** 15–20 seconds  
**Style:** Dark, cinematic, futuristic  
**Main objective:** Make people curious about GCPC without revealing the event's full details.

## 1. Scene-by-Scene Script

### Scene 1 — The Mystery Begins (0–3 seconds)

**Visuals:**

- Start with a completely dark screen.
- Add a subtle computer cursor or a brief digital glitch.
- Display the text in the center of the screen.

**On-screen text:**
“Something is coming…”

**Voice-over (optional):**
“You think you've seen it all?”

**Sound:**
A low electronic sound begins, creating a mysterious atmosphere.

### Scene 2 — Building Curiosity (3–7 seconds)

**Visuals:**

- Show quick shots of someone typing code.
- Include close-ups of a keyboard, computer screens, and digital patterns.
- Use fast cuts and glitch transitions.
- Keep the images brief so the audience cannot understand the full concept yet.

**On-screen text:**
“Think again.”

**Voice-over (optional):**
“Because we're preparing something different.”

**Sound:**
The music gradually becomes more intense.

### Scene 3 — A Glimpse of What's Coming (7–13 seconds)

**Visuals:**

- Show silhouettes of people collaborating or working together.
- Add quick flashes of futuristic graphics.
- Reveal the letters “GCPC” briefly, without showing the complete event details.

**On-screen text:**
“One event. New possibilities.”

**Voice-over (optional):**
“GCPC is getting closer. Are you ready?”

**Sound:**
Use a short build-up followed by a digital impact sound.

### Scene 4 — The Final Reveal (13–18 seconds)

**Visuals:**

- Display the GCPC and GDG Algiers logos.
- Use a clean, dramatic transition.
- End with a dark background and a final message.

**On-screen text:**
“Stay tuned. The reveal is coming.”

**Optional final line:**
“You won't want to miss this.”

**Sound:**
End with a strong electronic hit, then fade to silence.

## 2. Visual Direction

- **Colors:** Black or dark navy, with electric blue and white accents.
- **Typography:** Bold, modern sans-serif fonts.
- **Transitions:** Glitch effects, quick cuts, flashes, and smooth digital transitions.
- **Music:** Mysterious electronic music that builds in intensity.
- **Editing:** Keep the pace fast and avoid showing any shot for too long.
- **Branding:** Use the official GDG Algiers and GCPC logos and follow the event's visual identity.

## 3. Caption for Social Media

Something is coming… 👀

A new experience is getting closer, and we're not ready to reveal everything just yet.

Stay tuned. You might want to be part of this.

#GCPC #GDGAlgiers #StayTuned #ComingSoon

## 4. Important Note

The purpose of this teaser is to create curiosity, not to explain the event. Avoid revealing the full program, speakers, activities, or other details that have not yet been officially announced.

# TEASER 2: “WHAT DO YOU THINK GCPC IS?”

**Concept:** Interactive, playful, and community-focused  
**Duration:** 15–20 seconds  
**Style:** Energetic, modern, engaging  
**Main objective:** Encourage viewers to guess what GCPC is and interact with the post.

## 1. Scene-by-Scene Script

### Scene 1 — Ask the Question (0–3 seconds)

**Visuals:**

- Start with a large question mark on the screen.
- Use a quick zoom-in or a pop-up animation.
- Keep the background simple but visually interesting.

**On-screen text:**
“What do you think GCPC is?”

**Voice-over (optional):**
“We've got a question for you…”

**Sound:**
Use a short, energetic digital sound to grab attention.

### Scene 2 — Give Them Clues (3–8 seconds)

**Visuals:**

- Show quick shots of code, computer screens, technology, and people collaborating.
- Display one word at a time using animated text.
- Use quick transitions between each clue.

**On-screen text:**
“Technology?”
“Community?”
“Something bigger?”

**Voice-over (optional):**
“A new challenge? A new experience? Or something you haven't guessed yet?”

**Sound:**
Keep the music energetic and use short sound effects for each new clue.

### Scene 3 — Almost Revealing It (8–14 seconds)

**Visuals:**

- Display a countdown from 3 to 1.
- Show a few quick flashes of the GCPC logo or event-related visuals.
- Stop just before revealing the complete concept.

**On-screen text:**
“The pieces are coming together…”

**Voice-over (optional):**
“You're getting closer. But there's still more to discover.”

**Sound:**
Build the music toward the countdown, then stop it suddenly for a moment of suspense.

### Scene 4 — Invite the Audience to Participate (14–19 seconds)

**Visuals:**

- Display the GCPC and GDG Algiers logos.
- Use a clean background with readable text.
- End with a question to encourage comments.

**On-screen text:**
“What's your guess? Comment below!”

**Voice-over (optional):**
“Tell us what you think GCPC has in store. Stay tuned!”

**Sound:**
End with a short, upbeat digital sound.

## 2. Visual Direction

- **Colors:** Use the official GDG Algiers and GCPC colors. A blue-purple gradient can be used if it fits the event's visual identity.
- **Typography:** Large, bold, readable text.
- **Transitions:** Pop-up animations, zooms, quick cuts, and countdown effects.
- **Music:** Energetic electronic music with a playful, curious mood.
- **Editing:** Make the video feel interactive, with each clue appearing quickly.
- **Branding:** Keep the logos visible in the final scene and maintain consistent branding.

## 3. Caption for Social Media

We've given you a few clues… 👀

But can you guess what GCPC has in store?

Is it technology, a new challenge, a new experience, or something completely different?

Drop your guesses in the comments. Let's see who gets it right! 👇

Stay tuned for more.

#GCPC #GDGAlgiers #GuessTheEvent #ComingSoon

## 4. Important Note

This teaser should encourage interaction while keeping the mystery alive. The clues should create curiosity without revealing details that are intended for the official announcement.
